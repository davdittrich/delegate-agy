<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** pluralization of "fallback".

**Noun now primary:** the *bounded invocation*, not the `timeout` binary. The documents still describe a world in which the binary's presence is the question — README's environment-variable rows all end by naming what happens when it is absent, and PROJECT.md's Out of Scope entry treats "the unbounded fallback" as a thing that exists and is being kept.

**Decision: `promote`.** The prose must be rewritten around the bounded invocation, with mechanism selection as a footnote about *which* kill you get, not *whether* you get one. This is not `add-alongside`: no document keeps a sentence conditioned on the binary's presence for the *existence* of a bound.

**Test encoding the generalized intent:** case RB03 (plan 01-05) compares README's quoted literals against the constants defined in both scripts, so a document that drifts from the shipped behaviour fails the suite rather than quietly misleading a reader.
</assumption_delta_decision>
