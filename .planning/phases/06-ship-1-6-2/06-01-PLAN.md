<objective>
Close `delegate-agy-sup` (D-08): the `RB24` trap-preservation flake. Move `run_bounded`'s watchdog-arm timer teardown to *after* the host's TERM/INT/HUP dispositions are restored, closing the window in which a signal arriving during teardown fires `_rb_relay`, exits the shell, and skips the restore entirely.

This is the phase's **tracer**. It is the only fix whose root cause is `[ASSUMED]` rather than verified (RESEARCH.md ran 2 full suites plus 80 isolated iterations without reproducing the flake), and it is the fix that touches every layer this phase touches: both shipped scripts, the test-side script, and the harness. If the forcing test cannot be made red against the unmodified helper, hypothesis A1 is wrong and the phase needs replanning — and that must surface on commit 1, not commit 10.

Purpose: a host that installs a cleanup trap before its first bounded call must get that trap back, every time, or a later Ctrl-C kills the script's temp directory with the user's full prompt still in it (the in-file comment at `agy_bridge.sh:325-329` states exactly this rationale).
Output: a three-line statement reorder replicated byte-identically into three files, plus `RB30`, a deterministic forcing case proven red before the fix and green after.
</objective>
