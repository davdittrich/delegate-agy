  Decided by: simplicity — of the options that can actually answer the question, this needs one bounded call and one exit-code read.
- **Signalling the `models` subcommand instead**: far cheaper, non-generating, no quota at all. Rejected in D-12 on a correctness ground: `models` may not share the delegation path's process tree, so a clean death there would not license R11's `-k` on delegations.
