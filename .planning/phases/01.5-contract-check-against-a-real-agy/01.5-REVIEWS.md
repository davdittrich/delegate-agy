# Cross-AI Review — Phase 1.5 (plans)

## Codex Review

### Summary

The phase is thoughtfully scoped and grounded in the correct `fix/agy-bridge-resilience` worktree. The fixture-driven fake, bounded real-binary probes, disjoint exit codes, and documentation provenance are strong architectural choices.

However, several plans contain implementation-level contradictions that would prevent the phase from reliably meeting the ROADMAP criteria. The most serious are:

- the policy-binding probe cannot distinguish actual tool execution from fabricated output and does not create the competing-`GEMINI.md` condition it claims to test;
- the model-argument probe tests a display name but not a valid id, so it cannot classify "ids, display names, or both";
- the auxiliary `empty-success-capture` row incorrectly makes exit 0 practically unattainable;
- the ledger cannot print an accurate billed count in its header under the specified print-as-you-go design;
- CC03's self-scan is likely to classify its own mutation fixtures as real invocations;
- CC06 cannot inject its synthetic lists into the proposed hard-coded `_cc_expect_model`;
- the free bridge probe's scratch PATH lacks the tools needed to execute the bridge.

Overall risk: **HIGH until the plan contracts are corrected**. These are plan defects rather than problems in the underlying phase design.

---

### Plan 01.5-01

**Summary:** A useful tracer slice — establishes the operator command, third `run_bounded` copy, verdict space, first fixture, and absent-binary test before adding expensive probes.

**Strengths:** correct source of the third bounding copy (`scripts/agy_bridge.sh:17`, `:47-352`); RB02 widening is mechanically appropriate (`_rb_extract`, `tests/run-tests.sh:1202-1210`); exit codes 0/10/11 disjoint from the published bridge codes (`README.md:226-233`); CC01's full PATH replacement is appropriate; copying SH11's external safety bound is correct (`tests/run-tests.sh:1046-1067`).

**Concerns:**
- **HIGH** — the header/row architecture cannot later report the actual billed count. `_cc_row` prints immediately; the header must precede the rows and contain the billed-call count, which isn't known until Plans 03 and 05 finish their probes.
- **MEDIUM** — "raw bytes" and shell-text handling need a precise contract; command substitution strips trailing newlines.
- **LOW** — the tracer's main task is marked autonomous despite a hard external precondition (live authenticated binary).

**Suggestions:** buffer ledger rows and compute billed count/status before printing; distinguish raw fixture bytes from normalized evidence strings; split "implement tracer" from "capture live fixture."

**Risk:** MEDIUM.

### Plan 01.5-02

**Summary:** The hung-binary runtime test is well chosen, but CC03's proposed self-referential source scan is too brittle and not equivalent to the RB01 precedent it cites.

**Strengths:** CC02 reuses the fake's real SIGTERM-ignoring hang; external `timeout 30` correctly distinguishes internal return from the outer net (`tests/run-tests.sh:1046-1067`); fd-9 capture follows the documented safeguard (`:152-178`); the occurrence-floor/mutation intent is valuable.

**Concerns:**
- **HIGH** — CC03 will scan its own mutation fixtures and explanatory strings as though they were executable invocations. Unlike RB01, which scans separate shipped scripts, CC03 scans `tests/run-tests.sh` itself.
- **HIGH** — the "same logical line contains PATH=" rule is not a reliable shell execution model.
- **MEDIUM** — the scan's accepted forms conflict with its mutation requirements; doing this correctly with grep over Bash source approaches writing an incomplete shell parser.

**Suggestions:** route every harness invocation through one dedicated helper and statically require its use; keep mutation samples out of the scanned region, or scan a delimited production region only; prefer a runtime tripwire.

**Risk:** HIGH.

### Plan 01.5-03

**Summary:** The preflight and model fixture capture align well with the production model-resolution path. Main weaknesses are portable format validation and invalid-model capture semantics.

**Strengths:** mirrors production normalization/matchers (`scripts/agy_bridge.sh:507-525`); treats a no-`gemini-` list as degraded, not a class miss; separate stdout/stderr capture is appropriate; preflight-before-billing is a good cost safeguard; avoids the skip-permissions path (`scripts/agy_bridge.sh:679-682`).

**Concerns:**
- **MEDIUM** — the "exactly one TAB" expression is underspecified/non-portable.
- **MEDIUM** — `invalid-model.txt` cannot simultaneously be a byte-verbatim capture and an unspecified combination of stdout and stderr.
- **MEDIUM** — the billed counter infers generation rather than counting generating-capable calls attempted.
- **LOW** — format validation permits an empty display name.

**Suggestions:** use `$'\t'` or `awk -F'\t' 'NF==2'`; store invalid-model stdout/stderr in separate fixture sections; count every launched generating-capable call conservatively.

**Risk:** MEDIUM.

### Plan 01.5-04

**Summary:** Fixture-driven fake behavior is the strongest part of the phase. Copy-site analysis is accurate. CC06 cannot work with the helper signature currently specified.

**Strengths:** the five-copy-site inventory is correct (`tests/run-tests.sh:62`, `_purebin` at `:118`, and later sites); preserving RB27's bare environment is correct; replacing the canned list is necessary (R2/R4 pin `gemini-3.6-flash-high` at `:354-359`/`:460-477`); leaving `config/model-map.json` untouched is correct (maps aliases to classes, S6 enforces it at `:257-284`); loud failure on missing/empty fixtures matches production's degraded-list rejection (`scripts/agy_bridge.sh:512-518`).

**Concerns:**
- **HIGH** — CC06 cannot inject its synthetic lists into `_cc_expect_model` as specified. `_cc_expect_model CLASS` reads the fixed path `$HERE/fixtures/agy-models.tsv`; no fixture-path argument or override mechanism is provided.
- **MEDIUM** — `_cc_fixtures_beside` needs idempotent destination handling.
- **MEDIUM** — stripping every line beginning `#` is broader than stripping the defined provenance header.
- **LOW** — the plan overstates "no assertion anywhere pins a model id literal" (R3/R3b/R3c/R3d remain deliberate synthetic pins).

**Suggestions:** define `_cc_expect_model CLASS [FIXTURE]` defaulting to the real fixture; make `_cc_fixtures_beside` replace the destination deterministically; define an explicit fixture-header terminator.

**Risk:** HIGH.

### Plan 01.5-05

**Summary:** Addresses the hardest and most valuable questions, but contains the phase's most serious validity problems.

**Strengths:** reusing one call for related observations stays within the three-call ceiling; calling agy directly for display-name testing is necessary (the bridge validates `--model` against the id column first, `scripts/agy_bridge.sh:528-531`); the SIGTERM plan correctly discriminates coreutils from the fallback watchdog; every inconclusive result records payload shape and bound; the plan correctly refuses to "fix" contradicted behavior in this phase.

**Concerns:**
- **HIGH** — the `gemini-md-binds` probe cannot distinguish actual tool execution from fabricated output — asks the model to run a command and report output, classifies reply text with no observable tool side effect specified.
- **HIGH** — the probe does not recreate the competing-context condition the success criterion asks about (whether the policy binds *when agy can also see `GEMINI.md` files in other projects*) — no adversarial/distinguishable external `GEMINI.md` is placed anywhere agy can see it.
- **HIGH** — the free bridge precheck's PATH is insufficient — a scratch dir holding only the fake `agy` lacks the tools (`dirname`, `readlink`, `cut`, `sort`, `tail`, `mktemp`, …) the bridge needs before it ever reaches the point of invoking `agy`.
- **HIGH** — `model-arg-accepts` does not answer "ids, display names, or both" — only a display name is tested against real agy; a valid id is never separately submitted and merged into the row's evidence.
- **HIGH** — the eighth auxiliary row (`empty-success-capture`) corrupts exit-code semantics — in the normal case where the policy binds and the permission gate doesn't fire, that row is `unverified`, so a run where every real assumption verified still exits 10, contradicting D-06's "0 when every assumption is verified."
- **HIGH** — the billed count cannot be printed accurately with Plan 01's immediate-output ledger (same architectural gap as Plan 01's finding).
- **MEDIUM** — "increment only when agy actually generated" is not reliably observable.
- **MEDIUM** — the SIGTERM inference is more fragile than presented (process-group-dependent).

**Suggestions:** redesign D-10 around observable effects (a competing `GEMINI.md` with a unique marker, a secret file the forbidden tool would need to read, classification by filesystem/tool transcript rather than reply text alone); build the free-probe PATH from the same explicit whitelist as `_purebin`; define `model-arg-accepts` as an aggregation of two real observations (one id call, one display-name call); exclude `empty-success-capture` from verdict counters; buffer all rows and print only after billed count and final status are known.

**Risk:** HIGH.

### Plan 01.5-06

**Summary:** The documentation closeout is appropriately evidence-driven, but CC05 over-couples independent fixtures to one version/date, and the plan cannot guarantee criterion 4 when the preceding probe is inconclusive.

**Strengths:** updating README from the recorded ledger rather than the predicted answer is exactly right; the current stale troubleshooting advice is real (`README.md:229`); keeping the check outside the release gate is consistent with Phase 6's gate; correcting S5's stale blocked text is necessary (`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md:77`, `:93`); RB03 is a good precedent for literal pinning; respects the worktree/main-tree boundary correctly.

**Concerns:**
- **HIGH** — CC05 requires every fixture to share one version and capture date. Fixtures are produced by different probes and can legitimately be recaptured independently or across a version bump; requiring all `tests/fixtures/*` headers to equal one date/version blocks valid partial recapture or encourages false provenance.
- **HIGH** — criterion 4 is not guaranteed if `model-arg-accepts` remains unverified — the plan documents whatever the ledger found, but the ROADMAP requires README to state the fact as verified; an inconclusive probe should block phase completion rather than mark documentation done.
- **MEDIUM** — CC05's "delete any fixture → red" mutation conflicts with the conditional fixture contract (`empty-success.txt` is explicitly conditional).
- **MEDIUM** — the negative stale-string scan may not cover all misleading forms.
- **LOW** — editing ROADMAP in this plan broadens `files_modified` overlap more than needed.

**Suggestions:** pin README's version/date to the specific fixture(s) that support each claim rather than requiring global equality; introduce a required-fixture manifest; add a phase gate blocking criterion-4-complete unless `model-arg-accepts` is conclusive.

**Risk:** MEDIUM-HIGH.

### Cross-plan dependency and performance assessment

The six-wave chain is safer than necessary but not entirely artificial. Plan 01 must precede 02/03; after Plan 01, Plans 02 and 03 touch different files and could be developed in parallel (though concurrent edits in one worktree would be operationally unsafe); Plan 04 needs both 02's harness state and 03's fixtures; Plan 05 needs 04's fixture-aware fake; Plan 06 needs 05's recorded verdict. Logical graph: `01 → {02,03} → 04 → 05 → 06`. Strict serial waves are reasonable for a shared worktree but stronger than the file dependencies alone justify.

### Billed-call budget assessment

Nominal maximum remains three real generating-capable calls. The ledger's "actual billed" count is unreliable because it infers provider billing from outcomes rather than reporting launched calls.

### Exit-code assessment

The numeric selection (0/10/11) is consistent and safely disjoint from R5's codes. The implementation contract is inconsistent because Plan 05 makes the non-assumption `empty-success-capture` participate in status calculation — that row should be removed from verdict counters.

### Overall Risk Assessment

**HIGH.** The phase architecture is strong, and Plans 01, 03, 04, and 06 contain substantial good work. The current plans do not yet guarantee the four ROADMAP criteria. Before execution, the policy probe, model-form classification, ledger buffering, auxiliary-row status handling, CC03 scan, CC06 helper interface, and fixture provenance rules should be revised.

### Consensus Summary

**Cross-round.** The first round (antigravity, decision record) and this round (codex, plans) reviewed different artifacts and found no overlapping claims — expected, since planning hadn't started when the first round ran.

**Agreed strengths (within this round, corroborated by direct source verification):** the RB02/`_rb_extract` widening, SH11-shaped external bound reuse, the D-14a copy-site plumbing, and leaving `config/model-map.json` untouched are all independently confirmed correct.

**Highest-priority concerns:** F7 (exit-0 unreachable on a healthy run) is the single most damaging finding — it breaks the check's own headline contract. F1 (header/billed-count architecture), F2 (free-probe PATH), F3 (CC06 helper interface), F4 (CC03 self-scan), F5 (competing-context gap), F6 (model-arg-accepts single-form), and F8 (fixture over-coupling) are all independently confirmed and touch five of the six plans (01, 02, 04, 05, 06). Plan 03 carries only MEDIUM findings (not independently re-verified by the orchestrator; carried forward as plausible).

**Recommendation:** these are plan-level implementation defects, not decision-level (CONTEXT.md) defects — the underlying phase design is sound. Replan via `/gsd-plan-phase 1.5 --reviews` rather than proceeding to `/gsd-execute-phase 1.5` as written; several of these would cause the phase to fail its own acceptance criteria or produce misclassified verdicts if executed as-is.
